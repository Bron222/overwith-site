Reminders

My reminder is fashionably late.

To save battery, your device may group reminders together, so they can arrive a few minutes after the set time.

My reminders aren't showing up at all.

Check that notifications are allowed for Overwith in your device settings. On Android, battery-saving settings can also delay reminders.

The task name is hidden on my lock screen.

That's on purpose. Your device is set to hide sensitive notification content, and Overwith follows that setting. Unlock your device to see the full reminder.

Your tasks

What happens to the things I didn't finish?

They move forward automatically, so nothing gets lost. Day tasks move to today, "this week" tasks move to the current week, and weekly repeating tasks move to their next scheduled day. Any reminder moves with its task. A small tag, like "from Mon", shows where it started.

How do I move a task to another day?

Tap "Later this week" on a task to move it to "Sometime this week". To pin a "this week" task to a day, pick the day at the top, then tap "Do it" on the task. For any other date, tap the task and change it.

Can I see my tasks on another device?

Not right now. Tasks are stored only on the device where they were created.

The app

How do I turn the sounds off?

Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right and choose "Turn sound off". Overwith also stays quiet when your phone is on silent.

Can I use light mode?

Yes. Overwith starts in dark mode. Tap the ⋮ menu and choose "Switch to light mode". It remembers your choice.

Your data

How do I delete everything?

Uninstalling Overwith deletes all of its data from your device. See the Privacy Policy for the details.
